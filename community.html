About the researcher

Why this project exists

This project began as undergraduate research into whether digital connectivity actually translates into meaningful financial inclusion in Pakistan.

The goal was to use machine learning as a lens for a socioeconomic problem and make the findings accessible beyond an academic PDF.

This is where you come in

The data can show us the gap. It cannot tell us why.

We know many people have access but do not use digital finance. What we do not know from a spreadsheet is what is actually stopping them.

If you know someone in this position, or you have been in it yourself, your story helps make the numbers human.
